# clinicOS tester guide

For UAT clinics. Short and practical. Support: `<support contact, set by the PO>`. Feedback: `<feedback channel>`.
(The same text appears in the app's Help page.)

## Before you start

- Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone/iPad, or any current desktop browser.
- Add the app to your home screen: Android, tap Install when offered; iPhone/iPad, Share > Add to Home Screen and turn on
  "Open as Web App". Sign in once inside the installed app.
- Use real patients only if you are comfortable; the data is protected as described in the Privacy page. Do not enter
  anything you would not enter in your normal clinic software.

## Owner: first day

1. Open the signup link and create your clinic with the code we sent you (one code per clinic; do not share it).
2. Enter your clinic details; add one doctor and one receptionist with the Staff screen. They get an invite by e-mail;
   the link sets their password.
3. Set up AutoPay when asked. A Rs 5 test authorisation is taken and returned automatically. Nothing is charged during the
   trial period shown on the Billing page.
4. Check the Billing page shows your trial end and the date your first charge would start.

## Receptionist: a normal day

1. Today board: register a new patient or search an existing one; book or add a walk-in; check the patient in.
2. Take payment when the patient pays and print or share the receipt.
3. If the network drops, wait for the banner to clear and press Retry. Do not press the main button twice.

## Doctor: a normal consult

1. Open the patient from Today; the consult screen opens with vitals and history.
2. Search a diagnosis and medicines; the prescription is saved as you type.
3. Print or share the prescription PDF to WhatsApp.

## Nurse

Record vitals from the Today board; you do not see billing.

## Things worth trying and telling us about

- Switch user, sign out, and open the app again: no patient should be visible after signing out.
- Go offline in the middle of booking, then come back online.
- Use the app on the smallest phone you have.
- Anything that took more than three taps to find.

## What to send us

Tell us: what you were trying to do, what happened, which screen, and roughly when. A screenshot helps (hide patient
names). Never send us passwords, codes or patient details in the message.

## Quick help

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| No signup or reset code in the inbox | check spam; wait 2 minutes; ask the owner to resend; contact support |
| Locked out | use "Forgot password"; if you lost your second step, tell the owner or support |
| "Update available" message | save your work, reload; it never loses saved data |
| Reminder not sent | reminders need approved templates; ask support for the status |
