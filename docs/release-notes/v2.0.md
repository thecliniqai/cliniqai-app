# clinicOS v2.0 release notes (DRAFT for UAT start)

Status: draft written in V2-15 (15-06). Claude fills the "Known gaps" from the final evidence; the PO approves the text
before it is shared with UAT clinics. Plain language, no internal phase names in the version sent to testers.

## What is new for your clinic

- **Front desk.** A live Today board: register a patient, book, walk in, check in, take payment, all on a phone, tablet
  or desktop. Works on a flaky network without duplicating patients, visits or payments.
- **Consultation.** One consult screen for the doctor with vitals, history, diagnosis search and medicine search
  (CDCI medicine catalogue and an OPD diagnoses list), autosave, and a prescription that prints or shares as a PDF.
- **Roles.** Owner, doctor, receptionist and nurse each see only what their role needs. Sessions end after idle time
  and when you switch user; patient screens are cleared when you sign out.
- **Install as an app.** Add clinicOS to your phone or tablet home screen (Android and iPhone/iPad). It updates itself
  and tells you when a new version is ready.
- **Reminders.** Appointment reminders by WhatsApp once your templates are approved (MSG91). Delivery status is shown.
- **Subscription.** Free trial, then Standard at Rs 1,499 per month plus GST by AutoPay. UAT clinics join with a code and
  are not charged during UAT.
- **Your data.** Export your clinic's data at any time, including after a subscription lapses. Deletion requests are
  handled under the retention rules in the Privacy page.
- **Security.** Two-step sign-in options for staff, passwords checked against known leaks, and all patient data kept in
  India (Mumbai).
- **AI.** Shown as "Coming soon" on plans. The scribe appears as a clearly labelled **Preview** if your clinic opts in.
  Nothing is sold as AI yet.

## For the platform operator (not sent to testers)

- Production is a new Supabase Free project in Mumbai with encrypted twice-daily backups to AWS `ap-south-1`, an
  uptime monitor and ops alerts. Supabase Pro with point-in-time recovery follows at UAT exit.
- Email goes through Resend during UAT; SES follows after go-live.
- Live payments through Razorpay; UAT codes defer the first debit to the UAT exit date.

  the evidence with their margins.

## Rollback plan (operator)

1. **Front end:** Vercel > Deployments > the last good deployment > Promote to Production (instant rollback). Skew
   Protection keeps open tabs working.
2. **Database:** migrations are forward-fixed (expand, migrate, contract); never edit an applied migration. For a
   destructive mistake, restore from the latest FT-02 dump (`docs/runbooks/restore-drill.md` shows the path) after
   taking an on-demand dump of the current state.
3. **Payments:** pause new redemptions (campaign paused) and see `docs/runbooks/razorpay-live.md` section 8.
4. Record the rollback in the incident timeline (`docs/runbooks/incident.md`).
