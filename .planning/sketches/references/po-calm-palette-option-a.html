02 · Status & clinical meaning

Use colour semantically and consistently. Keep the majority of the interface neutral; status colour should help scanning, not decorate the screen.

Clinical safety: Reserve the danger palette for urgent, destructive or clinically important states. Allergy alerts should remain unmistakable and must never rely on colour alone.
