V2-DX round 2

Clinic Paper on a white canvas

Round 1 tinted the page. Round 2 removes the tint: the canvas and every working surface are white, structure comes from hairlines and spacing, and colour is spent on one accent and on meaning (queue state, allergy, money due). Two accent directions are shown below with the same neutrals, type and status colours. Values live in themes/clinic-paper.css (A) and themes/direction-b.css (B); every ratio is in v2-dx-evidence/contrast.md.

Shared neutrals

Ink #17171A for text, three greys for secondary text (all ≥ 4.5:1 on white), two hairlines (#EDEDEB, #E5E5E2) and one control border (#858580, 3.5:1). The only tinted surface is #F5F5F4 for wells such as keyboard hints and the segmented track.
