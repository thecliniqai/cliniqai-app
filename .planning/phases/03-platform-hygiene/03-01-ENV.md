## Deviations

1. **Rule 2:** with `NODE_ENV=production`, `APP_ENV` must be e2e/ci/staging/production; `development`/`test` would
   switch the fail-closed rules off on a deployment.
2. **`disabled` is non-retryable only for the SMS fallback.** `communication_queue_finish` has no non-retryable path
   and the plan allows no schema change, so a refused row is `failed` and retries (sending nothing) until
   `dead_letter`. Immediate dead-letter belongs to the V2-04 outbox.
3. `/api/ready` reports `ai` and `messaging` only: `EMAIL_PROVIDER` does not exist until 03-11b (handoff).
4. **Rule 1:** `verifyMsg91WebhookSecret` and `apiV1Enabled` treat an environment the schema refuses as "deny" (the
   full lane caught tests that stub `NODE_ENV=production` under `APP_ENV=test`).
5. Files outside the plan's list (none owned by 03-10): `lib/messaging/{dispatcher,types}.ts` (fallback skip,
   `retryable`), 13 test files + `tests/helpers/tenants.ts`, `tests/setup/teardown.ts`, `scripts/seed-local-dev.ts`
   (R-K rename), `tests/security/guard/engine.ts` (public-health probe may make the one `rpc:readiness_probe` call),
   `scripts/ci/env-example.mjs --root` for its spec.
6. Old key names in development without the new name produce "required (renamed from …)"; a developer's local
   `.env.local` must rename `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` / `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`.
