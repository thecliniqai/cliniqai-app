# Phase 03.1 Context: Doctor Cabin, Role-Based Access & Billing Invoicing (Phase 3A)

## Phase Boundary & Domain Scope
Phase 03.1 bridges the gap between administrative operations and clinical execution, while delivering full per-visit financial workflows. It ensures that in a single-doctor clinic, the Owner/Doctor has a frictionless clinical desk with 1-tap consultation launch, while multi-staff clinics benefit from strict, role-scoped station workflows (Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Biller). It also completes the provisional billing and payment recording requirement (REQ-billing-invoicing).

---

## Key Decisions & Architecture

### 1. Unified Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture
- **Decision D-29: Owner & Admin Unrestricted Default Access**
  - An authenticated user with role `owner` or `admin` has full, unrestricted access to ALL screens across the platform: Front Desk Dashboard, Live Queue, Calendar, Patients, the Doctor's Cabin (`/cabin`), Consultation Workspace (`/consultation/[id]`), Staff Management, Billing & Subscription, and Clinic Settings.
- **Decision D-30: Role-Scoped Dedicated Workspaces**
  - `doctor`: Directly lands on and focuses on **The Doctor's Cabin (`/cabin`)**. Clinical queue of their assigned waiting patients, active consultation banner, clinical histories, personal calendar slots. Management and subscription settings are hidden.
  - `nurse`: Dedicated Triage & Vitals entry station. Quickly captures BP, pulse, temp, SpO2, height, weight, blood sugar, allergies, and advances visit status to `Waiting (Vitals Ready)`.
  - `receptionist`: Front desk intake, patient registration, slot booking, walk-in token issuance, queue oversight, and checkout desk.
  - `biller` / cashier: Dedicated visit billing counter, itemized charge review, cash/UPI/card payment recording, and PDF receipt printing.

### 2. Clinic Profile Configuration (`/settings/clinic`)
- **Decision D-31: Practicing Owner Toggle**
  - Clinic Profile setting: *"Is the Clinic Owner a practicing doctor?"* (Boolean, defaults to `true` for small clinics).
  - When `true`: The owner is automatically registered in the treating doctor list (with medical registration number, specialty template, and digital signature). The sidebar includes both Operations AND the Doctor's Cabin.
  - When `false`: The owner is an administrator/investor who employs doctors; the Doctor's Cabin is hidden from their primary sidebar or available in supervisory mode.
- **Decision D-32: Clinic Operating Defaults**
  - Configurable default OPD consultation fees (New visit fee, Follow-up fee).
  - Accepted payment modes (Cash, UPI QR code, Card machine recording).

### 3. The Doctor's Cabin (`/cabin`)
- **Decision D-33: Dedicated Doctor's Cabin Route**
  - New top-level route: `/cabin`.
  - Features:
    - Real-time cabin queue: Displays only patients checked in and waiting for *this specific doctor*.
    - Patient card details: Token number, patient name, age/gender, vitals strip (pre-filled by nurse/receptionist), chief complaint/reason for visit, wait duration.
    - **1-Tap Action: "Start Consultation"**: With stethoscope icon, immediately sets visit status to `in_consultation` and navigates to `/consultation/[id]`.
    - **Active Consultation Banner**: If a visit is already in consultation, shows a prominent top banner: *"Currently consulting with [Patient Name] (Token #[X]) — Resume Consultation"*.
    - **Today's Completed Visits**: Quick review of prescriptions signed today, with ability to view PDF or reopen for amendment.

### 4. 1-Tap Consultation Launch across Existing Screens
- **Decision D-34: Queue & Dashboard Integration**
  - Live Queue (`/queue`): For patients in `waiting`, replace "Call In Doctor" with **"Start Consultation"** button that immediately navigates to `/consultation/[id]`. For `in_consultation`, provide **"Resume Consultation"**.
  - Dashboard (`/`): Replace generic "Manage" button in *Today's Active Patients* table with direct **"Consult"** action for checked-in patients.
  - Calendar Timeline (`/calendar`): Clicking an active visit card provides a direct **"Consult"** link.

### 5. Per-Visit Billing & Invoicing (REQ-billing-invoicing)
- **Decision D-35: Explicit Database Financial Schema**
  - Tables:
    - `invoices`: `id`, `tenant_id`, `visit_id`, `patient_id`, `invoice_number`, `subtotal`, `discount`, `tax`, `total_amount`, `status` (`draft` | `issued` | `paid` | `cancelled`), `created_at`.
    - `invoice_items`: `id`, `tenant_id`, `invoice_id`, `item_type` (`consultation` | `procedure` | `medicine` | `other`), `description`, `unit_price`, `quantity`, `total_price`.
    - `payments`: `id`, `tenant_id`, `invoice_id`, `amount`, `payment_method` (`cash` | `upi` | `card`), `reference_number` (e.g. UPI UTR number), `recorded_by`, `recorded_at`.
- **Decision D-36: Solo Doctor 1-Tap Sign-Off Billing**
  - When a solo doctor clicks `Complete & Sign` inside `/consultation/[id]`, the sign-off modal includes an inline billing checkout:
    - Pre-filled consultation fee (from clinic profile defaults, e.g. ₹500).
    - Quick payment recording chips: `[Cash] [UPI] [Card] [Unpaid / Pay Later]`.
    - In 1 click, the visit is completed, prescription sealed, payment recorded, and invoice generated.
- **Decision D-37: Reception / Biller Checkout Drawer**
  - In Live Queue (`/queue`), when a doctor completes a visit, status moves to `Billing / Ready for Checkout`.
  - Receptionist/Biller clicks `Collect Payment`: opens drawer displaying itemized charges, accepts Cash/UPI/Card, marks invoice `paid`, and issues receipt.
- **Decision D-38: Branded PDF Receipt & Collection Reports**
  - Server-side PDF receipt generator supporting A4 and thermal receipt formats with clinic branding and registration details.
  - Daily & monthly collection dashboard for clinic owner (`/billing/reports`): breakdown of Cash, UPI, and Card totals, billed visits vs unpaid, and CSV export.
