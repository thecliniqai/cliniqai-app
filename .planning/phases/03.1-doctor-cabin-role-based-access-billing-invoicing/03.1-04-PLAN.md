<objective>
Deliver clinic-branded PDF payment receipts and owner financial collection reporting:
1. Server-side React-PDF receipt document template (`receipt-document.tsx`) and generator (`generator.ts`) supporting A4 and compact POS slip layouts with clinic details, patient demographics, itemized charges, and payment method details (Cash, UPI UTR, Card).
2. Streaming authenticated route handler (`/api/invoices/[id]/receipt-pdf`) and in-app `receipt-modal.tsx` with print and download triggers.
3. Owner Collection Dashboard (`/billing/reports`) with real-time daily and monthly revenue breakdown by payment method (Cash vs UPI vs Card) and doctor breakdown.
4. Comprehensive integration tests verifying PDF generation, secure storage URLs, and financial aggregation accuracy.
</objective>

<threat_model>
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Description | Severity | Mitigation |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|----------|------------|
| T-03.1-07 | Information Disclosure | Receipt Route Handler | Cross-tenant access to payment receipts via guessable invoice IDs. | high | Enforce session validation, verify invoice belongs to authenticated tenant's ID, and issue time-limited (3600s) signed URLs. |
| T-03.1-08 | Privilege Escalation | Financial Reports Route | Non-admin staff (e.g. receptionist or doctor) viewing total clinic-wide owner revenue. | medium | Enforce role check: only `owner`, `admin`, and `biller` roles can access clinic-wide financial aggregation endpoints. |
</threat_model>
