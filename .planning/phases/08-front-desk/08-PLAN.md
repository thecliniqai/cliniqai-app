# V2-08 Front desk: plan

## Goal

A live, fast front-desk workspace. The receptionist, nurse and doctor land on one Today screen that updates by itself
(Realtime with a polling fallback), where check-in is one action, a walk-in is three keystrokes after typing a mobile
number, a booking is three or four actions, and a reschedule is four. Every staff write on that screen is an atomic
command in the V2-07 pattern. Patients are found and registered through one picker and one form (with duplicate
detection), slots are correct in the clinic's time zone for staff and for the patient's reschedule link, and a user with
two clinics can switch between them. The v1 dashboard, queue, cabin, calendar and booking screens are deleted as they are
replaced (no flags, PO-15).

### Visit state machine (R-7)

| From | To | Written by |
|---|---|---|
| booked | confirmed | `confirm_appointment` |
| booked, confirmed | waiting | `check_in_visit`, `start_consultation` (R-1) |
| no_show | waiting | `check_in_visit` (late arrival, R-8) |
| booked, confirmed, no_show | booked | `reschedule_visit` (new time; clears the confirmation; a no-show is rebooked here) |
| booked, confirmed | no_show | `mark_no_show` (only when the appointment time has passed) |
| booked, confirmed, waiting | cancelled | `cancel_visit` (a waiting visit that leaves) |
| waiting | in_consultation | `start_consultation` |
| waiting, in_consultation | completed | `sign_consultation`, `complete_and_bill`, `complete_visit` |
| completed (signed) | in_consultation | `reopen_visit_for_amendment` (the integrity guard of 000099 still demands the database snapshot) |
| in_consultation (reopened) | completed | `finalize_visit_amendment` |

Everything else is refused: nothing leaves `cancelled`; an unsigned `completed` never reopens; `in_consultation` cannot be cancelled.

### Carried in from the V2-07 merge (2026-11-21)
