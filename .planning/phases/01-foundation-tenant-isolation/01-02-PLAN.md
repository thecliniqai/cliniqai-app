## Assumption Delta Decisions

The phase-scope assumption-delta scan fired two signals. Both are recorded here (the scan is phase-level; this is the first executing plan).

| signal | term | noun now primary | decision | rationale |
|--------|------|------------------|----------|-----------|
| pluralization | "another" (cross-tenant criterion; multi-staff clinic) | the user↔tenant **membership** (many-to-many) | **promote** | `REQ-multi-tenancy` explicitly requires a data model in which a doctor may belong to multiple tenants. Promoting the M:N `memberships` model to the primary identity representation now means a future visiting-doctor feature is a data change, not a migration. The singular `users.tenant_id` shape is demoted to "the common case of one membership". The invariant is encoded as the unique `(user_id, tenant_id)` constraint plus the zero-membership error truth in this plan — the future phase that reintroduces a singular assumption turns that test red. |
| optional | "optional" (ABHA number/address) | the ABHA field anchor on the patient record | **no-change** | `REQ-compliance-data-layer` states the ABHA number is optional and nullable from day one by design; "optional" is the specified contract, not an anchor that moved. No promotion or alongside-addition is warranted. The boundary behaviour (NULL accepted, empty string rejected) is pinned as a truth in Plan 04. |
