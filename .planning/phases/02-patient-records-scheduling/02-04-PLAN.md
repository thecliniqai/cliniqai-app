    1. Implement `features/waitlist/actions.ts`:
       - `addToWaitlist`: requires full patient intake or existing patient selection, doctor preference, target date, notes.
       - `getWaitlistForDate`: returns active waitlist entries ordered by `priority DESC, created_at ASC`.
       - `promoteWaitlistEntry(entryId, scheduledAt)`: helper promoting specific entry.

       - Flips target visit `status = 'cancelled'`.

         - Marks waitlist entry as `status = 'promoted'`, `promoted_visit_id = newVisit.id`.
         - Records audit log entry `visit.auto_promoted_from_waitlist`.
