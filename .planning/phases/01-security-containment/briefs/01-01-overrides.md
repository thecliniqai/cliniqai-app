## 1. Binding rules (quoted)

- ADR-0005 D1 (`docs/adr/0005-confine-service-role-client.md:21-28`): "No export of a `"use server"` module, and no
  non-action module that can switch to the service role on a caller-supplied session or client, accepts the *acting*
  principal from the caller: no session, principal or DB client, no acting tenant/clinic id, no acting user/actor id,
  as a parameter or as an input field. The acting tenant and actor always come from `getContext()`/`requirePermission()`.
  *Target* ids are allowed … All `*Override` parameters are removed and `notifyPrescriptionIssued` is deleted (P0-01).
  Tests call domain services with an explicit context."
- ADR D2 (`:29-38`): one `getSystemDb(reason)` in `lib/supabase/system.ts` (`import "server-only"`), importable only
  from `lib/jobs/**` (internal part), `features/*/system.ts`, `app/api/{jobs,webhooks}/**`; one PHI-free log line per
  call; `features/*/system.ts` carry `import "server-only"`, never `"use server"`; Auth admin calls in
  `features/auth/system.ts` (C50); "Request-path code never falls back to the service role on error."
- ADR D3 (`:39-44`): public links → SECURITY DEFINER RPCs (P0-04); request-path enqueue → one SECURITY DEFINER enqueue
  RPC deriving tenant from `auth.uid()` (V2-04); `/api/ready` → health RPC (V2-03).
- ADR D4 (`:45-52`): ESLint only. `no-restricted-imports` confines `lib/supabase/system.ts`, `features/*/system`, the
  internal part of `lib/jobs`, and value imports of `createClient` (`@supabase/supabase-js`) / `createServerClient`
  (`@supabase/ssr`), owned only by `lib/supabase/{system,server,client}.ts` and `middleware.ts`;
  `no-restricted-syntax` bans identifier/string `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` outside the factory and `lib/env.ts`, and
  dynamic `import()`/`require()` of the factory. "`tests/**` and `scripts/**` are excepted from both rules". "A fixture
  per bypass, including an action importing a `lib/jobs` internal, proves each rule fires."
- ADR Phasing (`:53-65`): "Items 1 and the public-link part of 3 land in V2-01 (P0-01, P0-04); its inventory is
  generated by a tool at the V2-01 base commit … Items 2, 4 and the readiness RPC land in V2-03 (P1-34, P1-15)".

  `lib/api/**` until P1-37. "Every other current service-role importer moves … by V2-03." Adding paths needs an ADR

  and they must pass on the local stack both before and after the removal."
- ADR Verification (`:72-86`): V1 every `"use server"` export in the inventory: "an unauthenticated call with a forged
  override gets an authentication error (SEC-01)"; non-action modules: "the override parameter is gone and their only
