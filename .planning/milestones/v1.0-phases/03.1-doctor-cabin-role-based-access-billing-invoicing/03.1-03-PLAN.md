<objective>
Build the data layer and UI workflows for per-visit billing, consultation fee checkout, and payment recording (REQ-billing-invoicing):
1. Migration `000009_billing_invoicing_payments.sql` creating `invoices`, `invoice_items`, and `payments` tables with foreign keys and tenant isolation.
2. Server Actions and Zod schemas in `features/billing/` for creating invoices, itemizing consultation/procedure charges, and recording payments (`cash`, `upi`, `card`).
3. Integrated `sign-and-bill-modal.tsx` in `/consultation/[id]` enabling a solo doctor to seal consultation and record payment in one step.
4. Reception `checkout-drawer.tsx` in `/queue` enabling front-desk staff to collect payments for completed visits.
5. Integration test suite validating billing transactions and multi-tenant security.
</objective>

<threat_model>
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Description | Severity | Mitigation |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|----------|------------|
| T-03.1-05 | Financial Tampering | Payment Recording | User manipulating invoice amounts or recording payments for visits of another tenant. | critical | Validate all invoice totals server-side against authorized clinic pricing; enforce `tenant_id` verification on `invoices` and `payments` tables with Postgres RLS. |
| T-03.1-06 | Incomplete Transactions | Payment & Status Sync | Payment recorded without updating invoice status or audit log. | high | Wrap invoice and payment updates in atomic database transactions (`BEGIN...COMMIT`) or coordinated Server Actions. |
</threat_model>
