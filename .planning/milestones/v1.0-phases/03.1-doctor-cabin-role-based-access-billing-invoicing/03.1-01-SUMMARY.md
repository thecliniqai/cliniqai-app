## 1. Executive Overview

Plan `03.1-01` established the primary clinical workspace for outpatient practice: the **Doctor's Cabin** (`/cabin`), paired with dynamic role-based navigation and clinic profile practitioner settings. This resolves the fundamental outpatient clinic access gap:

1. **Role-Tailored Navigation & Landing:**
   - **`owner` & `admin`:** Full visibility across Operations (Doctor's Cabin, Dashboard, Live Queue, Calendar, Patients) and Management (Staff, Clinic Profile, Billing & Reports).
   - **`doctor`:** Directly routed to `/cabin` upon login, with focused navigation (Doctor's Cabin, My Calendar, Patients) and administrative clutter hidden.
   - **`receptionist` & `nurse`:** Operations workspace (Dashboard, Live Queue, Calendar, Patients) without access to private clinical or management cabins.

2. **Doctor's Cabin Workspace (`/cabin`):**
   - High-contrast **Active Consultation Banner** displaying the patient currently inside the cabin with 1-tap `Resume Consultation` linking directly to `/consultation/[id]`.
   - **Patient Queue Cards (`MyQueueCard`)**: Token badge, patient demographics, pre-filled triage vitals (BP, pulse, temp, weight), reason for visit, and 1-tap `Start Consultation` transitioning queue status to `in_consultation` and loading the workspace.
   - **Completed Consultations Strip**: List of today's concluded encounters with signed prescription status and quick links to view signed PDFs.

3. **Clinic Profile Practitioner & OPD Configuration:**
   - Practicing owner toggle (`owner_is_practicing_doctor`, defaults to `true`).
   - Default OPD consultation fee (e.g. ₹500) and follow-up fee (e.g. ₹300).
   - Accepted payment methods checklist (Cash, UPI QR, Debit/Card).
   - Backed by database migration `000009_billing_invoicing_payments.sql`.

4. **Automated Verification:**
   - Vitest suite `tests/cabin/navigation-roles.spec.ts` passing 100% (5/5 tests).
   - Full TypeScript compilation passing with 0 errors.
