## 4. Key Design Decisions

1. **Granular Sectional Insertion over Blanket Overwrite (D-07):** Doctors frequently dictate notes for only one clinical section (e.g. examination or advice). Providing discrete `[Insert Chief Complaints]`, `[Insert Examination Findings]`, `[Insert Clinical Advice]`, and `[Insert All Sections]` gives clinicians complete control over which fields are populated.
2. **Append/Replace Collision Safety (D-08):** If a clinician has already typed notes in a target field, inserting an AI draft immediately prompts `AppendReplaceModal` with a side-by-side diff. Doctors can append without erasing prior observations or replace when rewriting.
3. **Zero Autonomous Prescribing & Clean PDF (D-06):** In accordance with NMC 2023 telemedicine regulations, clinical drafts exclude brand dosages. The final printed/emailed prescription PDF contains zero AI badges or watermarks.
4. **Clinical Provenance Auditability (D-09):** When AI drafts are accepted, `metadata.ai_assisted = true` and `metadata.ai_sections_used = ['chief_complaints', 'examination', 'advice']` are recorded in the database `visits.metadata` column for audit and quality assurance.
